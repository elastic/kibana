# Using AI to Contribute to Kibana

We're glad you're here. AI tools can be a real help when you're contributing to Kibana, and we welcome the work they help you produce. A few things make the difference between a contribution that lands and one that stalls, so here's what we've learned works well.

Start by talking to us. Before you write any code, open an issue describing what you'd like to change, or leave a comment on one that already exists. This gives maintainers a chance to point you toward the right approach and saves you from building something that won't fit. A little conversation up front tends to save a lot of rework later.

Pick a change you can own from start to finish. The contributions that go smoothest are focused and self-contained, like fixing a specific bug, filling a testing gap, improving the docs, or making something more accessible. If you can validate the whole thing yourself, you're in a good spot.

Know your change inside and out. Read through the diff, run the code, and make sure you can explain why each part is there. AI can help you get to a working solution faster, but the understanding still has to be yours. When someone asks about a decision in your PR, you should be the one who can answer.

Think of AI as a tool that speeds up work you already understand, not something that does the thinking for you. Use it to move quicker on problems you can reason about, and steer clear of submitting anything you haven't read and tested yourself.

When you're discussing a change with maintainers, whether in an issue or a review, write in your own voice. Pasting model-generated replies into a conversation tends to be obvious, and it chips away at the trust that makes collaboration work. We'd much rather hear from you directly, even in a few rough words.

Here's the honest bottom line. PRs that show you understand your own work get our attention and our review. PRs that read like unreviewed AI output will be closed. We really do want your contributions, and we want you to succeed, so put your understanding into what you send us and we'll meet you there.
